Residential Services

At APW Electrical, we offer a wide range of domestic electrical services.

RCD Safety Switches / Home Safety Switches

In Western Australia, laws have been introduced preventing a property from being sold or rented unless there is a minimum of two RCDs (Residual Current Devices) in the property protecting both the lighting and power sockets.

Even though this law only applies when a property is to be sold, it is a prudent safety measure to have RCDs installed in all homes. Contact APW Electrical if you require RCD installation – it may just save your life.

Smoke Alarms

Western Australian law also prevents property from being sold or rented without mains-powered smoke alarms being installed. Smoke alarms are proven to save lives and should be installed in all homes.

Installation must be carried out by a licensed electrical contractor, with the smoke alarms needing to be changed every 10 years due to the lifespan of the detectors. APW Electrical specialises in the installation of smoke alarms and all our products meet or exceed Australian regulations.

Power Points

We install, repair and replace power points, faulty switches and lighting dimmers. We can help with all your power needs.

Not enough power points? We can help by installing additional socket outlets or changing existing ones from single to double or even quads. We also run power to sheds, spas and pools and can split your current circuits up, preventing overloads.

Telephone / Data Points / TV

We install and wire telephone, data and TV points to provide access and connectivity throughout your home.

Lighting and LED Downlights

At APW Electrical we can help you with the design, supply, installation and repair of all forms of lighting. There are many exciting innovations happening in the lighting field, including intelligent motion sensor lighting and wireless control systems.

APW Electrical promote LED downlights for new and old homes as they are a much more energy-efficient option than traditional lighting. We are able to supply, install and repair this safe, long-lasting technology.

Ceiling Fans

A ceiling fan is a simple and economical way to improve the heating and cooling efficiency of your home. We install a wide range of ceiling fans with or without lights, and remote control options are available.

APW Electrical can also repair or replace faulty ceiling fan switches and speed controls.

Fault Finding and Repairs

At APW Electrical we have the expertise and equipment to diagnose and fix all of your electrical problems.

What causes a power failure? The short answer is a lot of different things. Sometimes it can be moisture getting into the wrong places and causing a short, a common problem after heavy rains. Then there are faulty appliance failures; these can be the easiest to find. Simply unplug everything and switch the RCD back on, then slowly plug things back in until the power trips again.

We’ve had rats, mice and ants in switchboards, bugs and dirt in socket outlets, and on one sad occasion a cat in the roof. Dogs chew cords – thank goodness for RCDs. Wiring issues are more common in older houses where the installation is deteriorating due to age and climate. Simply put, it’s not always easy to work out where or what the fault is, but we will locate it!

Please note that we do not do appliance repairs – except for ovens.
