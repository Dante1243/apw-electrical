Commercial, Real Estate & Industrial

APW Electrical are ready to help you with all real estate, commercial and industrial electrical work.

What we do

Real Estate

Do you manage or own an investment property? We have a proven track record with local agents and fully understand the needs surrounding rental properties. We even offer yearly inspections of the RCDs and hard-wired smoke alarms, where we carry out testing and visual inspection to confirm the property’s ongoing compliance with current laws.

Industrial

We have worked with many industrial businesses in the Rockingham and Kwinana areas, helping them with their setups, additions or alterations. We are more than happy to give advice.

Commercial

We often work for commercial businesses, helping to set up, maintain or repair their power, lighting or data needs, with many returning customers. We have a good understanding of local legislation, putting us in a position to advise and help.

Commercial services

What we can help with

  • Fault finding and electrical repairs: residential, commercial and industrial
  • Electrical wiring of workshops, offices, and commercial and industrial premises
  • Switchboard upgrades and repairs
  • Installation of RCD safety switches, smoke alarms, power points, lighting and LED downlights, ceiling fans, and phone and data points
  • Energy efficiency and safety inspections
  • Testing and tagging services

The APW Electrical team are fully licensed and insured electrical contractors. We take pride in offering quality service and only use materials and parts we would be happy to have in our own homes.
